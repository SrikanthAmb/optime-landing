What Optime takes care of

Everything below happens on its own. You can see what was done, and why, on the Dashboard at any time.

Background programs

Ends programs running without a window that use a lot of memory or processor time. Apps you have open, or have used in the past week, are left alone.

Background services

Stops services that Windows or other software started and you don't use. With Full control on, this takes one Windows permission, once, and you can turn it off any time.

Startup apps

Turns off apps that start with Windows but you don't need, so your laptop is ready sooner after you sign in. Nothing is uninstalled.

Windows updates

Keeps your laptop responsive while updates install, by letting your apps go first.

Junk files and disk health

Clears temporary files and browser caches once a week, and checks your disk's health.

Wi-Fi to hotspot

If the Wi-Fi you're on drops or its internet stops, anywhere, Optime connects to your phone's hotspot. It stays on the hotspot until a Wi-Fi your laptop already knows is in range, like your home network when you get back, and then moves you to it. You can turn the hotspot off yourself any time to save your phone's data.

Battery

Tells you when the battery is full so you can unplug, loudly through the speakers, or at your own volume if headphones are in.

Daily health report

A short daily summary of what Optime did and how your laptop is doing, saved where you choose.

Never touched: Windows itself, your antivirus, and your hardware's own helpers for sound, graphics, touchpad, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

Questions

Can I undo something Optime did?

Yes. Open the Review tab, tick it under Ended by Optime and press Restore selected. Nothing is ever uninstalled or deleted.

How often will it ask me things?

Rarely. It asks on first run, when you install something new, and once about anything you brought back but haven't used for a week. Everything else it handles itself.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Without internet, Optime uses its built-in rules and switches back to AI mode when it can. It needs to check your license at least once every 7 days.

What happens when I get a new laptop?

On the old laptop, open License & Reports and click Deactivate this device. Then enter your key on the new one. No need to contact us.

Does it run on Mac?

Not yet. Optime runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11.

What does Optime send over the internet?

Your license check, and in AI mode the names and usage of background programs it needs to judge. Never your files, documents, messages, passwords or browsing. The privacy policy has the details.
