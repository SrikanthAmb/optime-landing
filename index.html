What it does without asking

Full autonomy, not a suggestion box

Memory & CPU

Deprioritizes CPU‑heavy processes instead of killing them, and closes memory hogs when things get tight — normalized fairly across every core you have.

Wi‑Fi failover

Notices when your home network drops, switches to your phone's hotspot, and switches back the moment home Wi‑Fi is reachable again.

Startup & junk

Trims bloated startup apps and clears out junk files on a schedule, so the laptop stays as fast on day 300 as it was on day one.

Battery saver

Warns you at 99–100% charge so you can unplug before it babysits the battery for you — full volume through the speakers, and left exactly as you set it if you're on headphones.

Questions

Before you install it

Will it ever interrupt me?

No. Optime doesn't ask permission for routine decisions — it acts and logs the reasoning. You can open the dashboard any time to see exactly what it did and why.

What happens if I move to a new laptop?

You can deactivate a device yourself from your account and activate Optime on the new one — no need to contact support for routine moves.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Core management keeps running offline. The AI reasoning layer needs a connection; without one, Optime quietly falls back to rule‑based decisions.

Windows, macOS, or Linux?

Optime runs on all three.
